Wavelength-Tuning Driver for a Microring Modulator

May 2026

National Undergraduate Innovation Project / Digital Modulator / Analog Circuits

  • Designed a driver architecture for wavelength locking and wide-range tuning in response to resonance drift in microring modulators.
  • Used a sigma-delta modulator and a 4-bit DAC to provide precise resonance-wavelength tuning and implementation-oriented control.
  • Compiled the final materials for a nationally funded undergraduate innovation project rated excellent at completion, documenting circuit design and system verification.
  • Repository: GitHub - Micro-ring-Wavelength-Locker

National Second-Prize Project, 10th IC Innovation Competition (Loongson)

Aug. 2026

Verilog / LA32R / FPGA / 1D-CNN / DMA

  • Developed an LA32R soft-core SoC for the Loongson enterprise challenge of the 2026 National College Student IC Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition.
  • Integrated a fixed-point 1D-CNN accelerator, DMA, external memory, and UART/Ethernet data paths for online and offline classification of 16,000-point current waveforms.
  • Ported the system to both the Loongson FPGA Cloud Artix-7 platform and the Z100/Zynq-7000 board; the Artix-7 offline board test achieved 30/30 correct classifications.
  • Repository: GitHub - la32r_soc
