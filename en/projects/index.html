ART: ASTF-Based RTL Transformation Agent

Nov. 2026

LLM / Verilog / Python / Vivado

  • Expresses natural-language circuit specifications as abstract signal transition functions (ASTFs).
  • Audits and repairs ASTFs, generates Verilog, then checks it with simulation and Vivado synthesis.
  • Across three benchmarks and six models, the rate of passing both simulation and synthesis rose from 80.1% to 89.5%.
  • Won national second prize in the National College Student Embedded Chip and System Design Competition.
  • Repository: GitHub (access required)

KWT-1 Keyword Spotting Accelerator on E203 SoC

Nov. 2026

Verilog / RISC-V / SoC / Transformer / FPGA

  • Integrated a fixed-point FFN1 accelerator with DMA, ping-pong buffers, and an 8×8 PE array.
  • Extended the design to a 12-layer Transformer encoder with attention, FFN, residual adds, and layer normalization.

  • Won national second prize in the National College Student Embedded Chip and System Design Competition.

Loongson LA32R SoC with a 1D-CNN Accelerator

Aug. 2026

Verilog / LA32R / FPGA / 1D-CNN / DMA
