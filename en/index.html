About Me

I am an undergraduate student in Integrated Circuit Design and Integration at Huazhong University of Science and Technology. My interests are end-to-end digital circuit design, AI for EDA, and LLM agents. Through projects involving SoC architecture, RTL implementation, and FPGA verification, I have become interested in how intelligent tools can improve chip design workflows. I hope to connect practical circuit design problems with automation methods and build systems and tools that can be rigorously verified.
